# Code of conduct

Conso is a small project with a simple rule: be the kind of person others are glad to work with.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.
- Give feedback on the work, never on the person.
- Accept that a decision may go another way than you hoped.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Discriminatory language or imagery of any kind.
- Publishing anyone's private information.
- Any behavior that would make a reasonable person feel unwelcome or unsafe.

## Scope

This applies everywhere the project lives: issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues, and block anyone who breaks these rules. To report a problem privately, use [private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/PoPs9444/conso-bar/security/advisories/new) and say it concerns the code of conduct.
